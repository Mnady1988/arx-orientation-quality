How the score works

After each orientation, a team member contacts at least one attending broker (ideally within 48 hours) and asks them to rate the session on the six criteria below, from 1 to 5. Ask the questions in the broker's words and don't let the salesperson who presented collect their own feedback.

The session score is the average of the six ratings converted to 100 (all 5s = 100, all 3s = 60). Salesperson and broker scores are the average of their session scores.
